# REGION REPORT: India — Outside Metros (Goa, Kerala, Rajasthan, Himalayan Touring Corridors, Northeast)

## Methodology
Searched: Moto Guzzi official dealer locator (blocked by CDN — access denied on all URL variants), CarAndBike.com dealer pages, ZigWheels, BikeWale, 91Wheels (all JS-rendered SPAs with no server-side dealer data), xBhp.com (India's largest motorcycling platform), Team-BHP (Cloudflare-blocked), Wikipedia, Piaggio India/Aprilia India/Vespa India sites (all CDN-blocked), Reddit r/motoguzzi and r/indianbikes, Instagram, Facebook Moto Guzzi India page, Rushlane, Overdrive India, Brave Search (captcha-blocked).

## India Distribution Model
Moto Guzzi operates in India through **Piaggio Group's "Motoplex"** multi-brand concept stores, which carry Vespa, Aprilia, and Moto Guzzi under one roof. The India importer is **Piaggio Vehicles Private Limited (PVPL)**, headed at the time of most documented launches by Mr. Stefano Pelle, Managing Director and CEO.

Moto Guzzi models sold in India (per xBhp 2016-10-26): Audace, ElDorado, California Touring 1400, California Touring Custom, Griso SE, V9 Bobber, V9 Roamer, MGX-21 Flying Fortress.

## Current Status (CarAndBike.com, Sep 2026)
**"There are approximately 2 Moto Guzzi bike dealers operating in India as of Sep 2026. Moto Guzzi bike showrooms in India are spread across 2 states and 2 cities."**

The two active cities confirmed on the page: **Chennai** and **Pune**.

This implies the Hyderabad and Kochi Motoplex stores have either closed or stopped carrying Moto Guzzi since 2016. **No Motoplex store has ever existed in Goa, Rajasthan, any Himalayan touring corridor (Manali/Leh), or Northeast India.**

## Independent Workshops / Parts Depots
**ZERO found** in the target region. Searched xBhp, Team-BHP, Reddit, and general web sources. No independent workshop with evidenced Moto Guzzi work was found in Goa, Kerala (outside Kochi), Rajasthan, the Himalayan touring corridors, or Northeast India.

## Owner Clubs / Knowledge Nodes
**ZERO found.** No national or regional Moto Guzzi owner club was identified for India on any source searched. The Moto Guzzi community presence in India appears extremely thin — no dedicated Facebook group, no Instagram community page, no forum sub-section found. xBhp (India's largest motorcycling community with22+ years) has Moto Guzzi as a brand tag but no active owner community section.

## Summary Assessment

India's non-metro touring corridors are a **complete desert** for Moto Guzzi support. The entire country has only2 confirmed dealers (Chennai and Pune), both in major metros far from the target touring regions. The historical4-store Motoplex network has contracted to2. No independent workshop anywhere in India outside the Motoplex network was found with evidenced Guzzi capability. No owner clubs exist to serve as knowledge nodes.

**For a Guzzi rider on the Golden Quadrilateral or Himalayan touring corridors**: there is no service node within realistic distance. The bike would need to be shipped to Chennai or Pune for any authorized service. The touring-support angle for this region is effectively non-existent.

**Bottom line: an honest empty result. The target region genuinely has nothing.**
