# GSN — next round (Nathan's list, 2026-09-10)

Captured verbatim-in-substance before a context clear; the next session
executes against this. Order below is Nathan's.

1. **Corridor mode.** Upload a GPX, get every node within N miles of the
   route, exportable back to the device. The touring rider's killer
   feature; Nathan has a 2027 test case with a very long route.
2. **Era competence.** Tag nodes by what they can actually touch:
   loop-frame, Tonti, spine-frame, CARC, small-block, V85, V100/PADS.
   The vintage crowd is the loudest part of the Guzzi universe and
   "full-service" doesn't tell them whether the shop has ever seen a
   Bing carb.
3. **The Desert Index.** Publish support-desert metrics — miles to
   nearest full-service by state and country, the ten worst gaps in
   America. Data-journalism story (Common Tread / Motorcyclist bait);
   a neutral finding, not a review, so it fits doctrine.
4. **Garmin GPI export** alongside GPX/KML. The Zumo people will love
   you specifically.
5. **Two dates per node**: last scraped vs. last confirmed by a rider,
   with method (web / phone / visited). Freshness is shown already;
   showing *how* fresh is the trust upgrade, and it makes the scraped
   Italian nodes honest about being thin.
6. **Frictionless reports.** PRs are a high bar for a 68-year-old with
   an Eldorado. A form that prefills a GitHub issue, or even a mailto
   with a templated body — no accounts, no backend, consistent with
   PRIVACY.md.
7. **Events layer.** GMG, MGNOC rallies, regional meets as time-bounded
   nodes. A reason to come back monthly instead of once per breakdown.
8. **Public GeoJSON endpoint** with a stable schema under the ODbL
   already chosen. What lets the Discord bot, the guzzibot, and other
   people's projects exist without us doing the work.
9. **A counter sticker.** QR to the site, offered free to every
   full-service node. Dealers become distribution; it's the only
   advertising they'll ever get for nothing.
